Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you get it there by this afternoon?

Sometimes. We will work it out from the collection point against lawful driving time and tell you honestly, including when the answer is no. A yes that depends on a driver running over hours is not a yes.

How much is my load insured for?

Goods in transit cover is stated before carriage, per consignment. If your load is worth more than the limit, tell us in advance and we will arrange cover rather than discovering the gap after an incident.

Do I need a full vehicle?

Usually not. Most consignments suit pallets or a part load, which is considerably cheaper. We will tell you when something is too fragile or awkward for shared transport.

Will my pallet be double-stacked?

In shared transport it may be, so it needs to be built for it. We will advise on packaging before collection rather than after damage.

Do you carry hazardous goods or waste?

Not unless agreed in writing beforehand. Hazardous goods require ADR and waste requires carrier registration, and we will not take either on casually.

What proof do I get?

Proof of delivery returned after every job, and updates during transit on dedicated work.

Send the weight, dimensions and both postcodes for a free written quote.
